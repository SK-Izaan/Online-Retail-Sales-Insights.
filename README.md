# 📊 Online Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

## Overview

This project is an end-to-end sales analysis of an Online Retail dataset using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective was to clean raw transactional data, analyze sales performance, build interactive dashboards, and summarize business insights for decision-making.

The project demonstrates a complete Excel-based analytics workflow including **Power Query**, **Pivot Tables**, **Pivot Charts**, **Slicers**, **Timeline filters**, dashboard design, and business insight reporting.

---

# Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Clean and prepare retail transaction data for analysis.
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify revenue trends over time.
- Compare domestic and international sales.
- Discover top-performing products.
- Summarize customer purchasing behavior.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business users.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

---

# Dataset Description

The dataset contains historical online retail transactions including invoices, products, quantities, prices, customers, and countries.

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Total Revenue | **$10.6M** |
| Total Invoices | **19,960** |
| Average Invoice Value | **$533.17** |
| Countries Served | **38** |
| Domestic Sales | **90%** |
| International Sales | **10%** |
| Peak Sales Season | **September – November** |

# Key Insights

## 1. Strong Overall Sales

- Total revenue reached **$10.6 million** across **19,960 invoices**.
- The average invoice value is **$533.17**, indicating relatively high-value transactions.

---

## 2. Domestic Market Dominates

- Approximately **90%** of total sales come from the domestic market.
- International markets contribute the remaining **10%**, indicating opportunities for global expansion.

---

## 3. Seasonal Sales Pattern

Sales activity increases significantly between:

- September
- October
- November

These months represent the strongest sales period, likely driven by seasonal purchasing patterns.

> **Note:** December 2011 data may be incomplete. Therefore, the observed decline in December sales should not be interpreted as a confirmed business downturn without complete monthly data.

---

## 4. Product Contribution

The following products generate a substantial share of revenue:

- DOTCOM POSTAGE
- REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER
- PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE

Together, these three products contribute approximately **47% of total sales**, highlighting a strong concentration of revenue among a small number of products.

---

## 5. Geographic Reach

The business serves customers across **38 countries**, demonstrating an international customer base despite domestic sales contributing the majority of revenue.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Increase inventory planning ahead of the September–November peak sales period.
- Expand marketing efforts in international markets to increase the current 10% share of sales.
- Monitor dependency on top-selling products and diversify the product portfolio where possible.
- Continue tracking customer purchasing patterns to identify opportunities for repeat business.
- Regularly refresh dashboards with updated data to support ongoing business decisions.
